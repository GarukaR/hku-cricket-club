DIRECTION 3 — “THE INNINGS”. Treats the club as a publication: the lead match report is the hero rather than a slogan, a numbered rail carries the week, and the photo mosaic runs on an asymmetric grid. Green is the only colour on the page, so it does all the signalling work.
The one direction of the three that fully supports both light and dark themes — try your system toggle. It also depends most on the club actually posting: strong when there is a report to lead with, thin when there is not.
COLOURS ARE NOW THE CLUB'S OWN — crest green leads and crest red signals results, in both the light and dark themes. The crest hexes were read off a phone screenshot of the logo, so they are close but not exact; the original vector file would let them be sampled properly. Fonts are system stacks because this sandbox blocks external fonts. The report, quotation and scores are invented sample content.
