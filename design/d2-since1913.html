The Club

The Club was formed in 1913, a mere two years after the University itself, and has fielded a side in almost every season since. Play is at Sandy Bay, the University's ground on the western shore, with league fixtures on Saturdays.

Membership is open to current students, staff and alumni of the University of Hong Kong. Experience is welcome but not required; a good number of the present side had never played before joining.

A university club is only ever eleven people wide and a hundred years deep. Club handbook, 1988

DIRECTION 2 — “SINCE 1913”. Structured as a printed university record rather than a magazine: centred masthead under a double rule, a results table treated as an archive, and marginalia carrying the standing facts. Restraint is the point — one accent (oxblood), one gilt detail (brass), everything else ink on oyster.
Deliberately cool oyster rather than warm cream, to keep it institutional instead of rustic. Commits to a single light world, so it does not offer a dark theme.
COLOURS ARE NOW THE CLUB'S OWN — taken from the crest, but carried down to the depth of printed ink, because solving only for the accessibility minimum returned bright modern colours that fought this direction. The crest hexes were read off a phone screenshot of the logo, so they are close but not exact; the original vector file would let them be sampled properly. Fonts are system stacks because this sandbox blocks external fonts. Results, the 1988 quotation and training times are invented sample content.
